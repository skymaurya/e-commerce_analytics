# 📊 E-Commerce Analytics – Executive Business Report  

---

## 🎯 Project Objective :

The objective of this project was to analyze end-to-end e-commerce performance across customers, sellers, products, payments, and operational efficiency using a structured data warehouse and business intelligence dashboard.  

The goal was to transform raw transactional data into decision-ready insights for executive stakeholders.  

---

## 📈 Executive Summary  :

### 1️⃣ Revenue & Growth Trends  

- 2017 showed strong and consistent revenue growth.  
- 2018 (Jan–Sep) shows a visible slowdown and declining trend.  
- Indicates possible market saturation, retention issues, or operational inefficiencies.  

**Business Impact:** Growth momentum has weakened and requires strategic intervention.  

---

### 2️⃣ Customer Behavior & Retention Risk  :

- Over 80% of customers are one-time buyers.  
- 57% of customers are classified as at-risk.  
- Majority of revenue comes from mid-value customers.  

**Business Risk:** Customer acquisition is strong, but retention is weak.  
Improving repeat purchase behavior can significantly increase lifetime value.  

---

### 3️⃣ Regional Revenue Concentration  :

- ~90% of revenue comes from South & Southeast regions.  
- SP, RJ, MG are the top-performing states.  
- RS and PR show emerging potential.  

**Business Risk:** Geographic revenue concentration increases exposure risk.  
Expansion strategy needed in underperforming regions.  

---

### 4️⃣ Seller Performance & Platform Risk  :

- 50% of revenue is generated by a small group of high-performing sellers.  
- 80% of sellers maintain acceptable delivery performance (<20% late rate).  

**Business Insight:** Seller concentration creates dependency risk.  
Retention and diversification strategies recommended.  

---

### 5️⃣ Product & Quality Insights  :

- High-rated products (4★+) generate maximum revenue.  
- Certain low-volume categories contribute disproportionately to late deliveries.  

**Operational Insight:** Product quality and fulfillment efficiency directly impact revenue performance.  

---

## 📌 Strategic Recommendations  :

- Launch retention & loyalty campaigns.  
- Improve fulfillment process for low-performing segments.  
- Expand geographically beyond core states.  
- Reduce revenue dependency on top sellers.  
- Promote high-performing product categories.  

---

## 🏁 Conclusion  :

This project demonstrates how structured data modeling and business analytics can transform transactional data into strategic decision support.
